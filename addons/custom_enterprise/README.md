Custom Enterprise Suite(C)
===========================

Custom Odoo Community implementations of Enterprise apps: Accounting+, Helpdesk
SLA/CSAT, Payroll & Appraisals, Approvals, DMS, Barcode Scan, QC, PLM, Sign,
Subscriptions, Rental, Marketing Automation, Social, Field Service, Planning,
KPIs & Studio-Lite.

Upgrade-safe custom Odoo 20.0 Community module delivering custom equivalents of
Odoo Enterprise capabilities without per-user license fees:

- **Accounting & Finance**: Fixed Assets & Depreciation schedules, Deferred
  Revenue/Prepaid Expense schedules, GL/Analytic Budgets, Bank CSV Import &
  Rule-based Auto-Reconciliation.
- **Helpdesk+**: Support Teams (round-robin), SLA Policies & Breach Tracking,
  Canned Responses, and Customer CSAT Ratings.
- **HR Payroll & Appraisals**: Indian salary structures/rules, Payslip runs,
  Accounting Journal Entry posting, PDF Payslips, and Employee Appraisals.
- **Approvals & DMS**: Multi-step Approval Requests and central Document
  Management folders with expiry alerts and 1-click Bill/Ticket/Approval/Sign.
- **Barcode, Quality Control & PLM**: Interactive Barcode Scan Console for
  pickings & cycle counts, QC Checkpoints with picking validation guards, and
  Garment/Gadget Tech Packs & Engineering Change Orders (ECOs).
- **Sales, Services & Marketing**: e-Signature portal (`/my/sign/<token>`),
  Subscriptions & recurring auto-invoicing, Rental bookings, Drip Marketing
  Automation, Social Post Calendar, Field Service Tasks, and Shift Planning.
- **Executive KPIs & Studio-Lite**: Cross-ERP KPI snapshots and safe guided
  `x_studio_*` custom field builder.
